## Work Distribution 

**How we assign tasks:** 

Tasks are created and tracked using GitLab Issues. Each task must:

- Have a clear title and description
- Be assigned to one team member
- Include labels
- Be linked to a milestone or sprint
- Be updated regularly with progress comments

**Issue workflow**
Backlog → In Progress → Review → Done

**What if someone can't complete their task:** 

Potential risks were mentioned in the daily meeting.

**Code review process:** 

All code reviewed by at least one other person 

Review before merging to main branch 

**Reviewer must give at least one meaningful comment for each merge request.**

## Team Ground Rules

**We agree to:** 

1. Communicate respectfully, focusing on solutions.  

2. Complete tasks within the agreed timeline and take responsibility for assigned tasks.

3. Keep important matters communicated in a timely manner. 

4. Encourage a culture of knowledge sharing, collaboration, and continuous improvement within the team. 

**We will NOT:** 

1. Dismissing or disrespecting others' feedback, or negating team members' contributions. 

2. Delaying tasks without justification or missing deadlines without explanation. 

3. Merging code without prior review. 

**Conflict Resolution** 

**If we disagree, we will:** 

1. Clarify the disagreement first in simple words in our group meeting. 

2. Discuss our opinions in group together. 

3. We made the decision based on the majority vote and log decision. 

**Escalation path (when to involve instructor):**

1. Discuss the issue again during the next team meeting.
2. Seek advice from the tutor or teaching assistant.
3. If the issue persists, escalate to the course instructor.

**Quality Standards**

- Follow the project coding conventions
- Include basic comments and documentation
- Be committed to GitLab

Review:
