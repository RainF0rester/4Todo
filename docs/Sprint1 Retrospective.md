## 2. Set the Stage

### Review Meeting Basic Principle (Prime Directive)

Regardless of what problems we identify in this retrospective, we understand and trust that each team member did their best given the knowledge, skills, resources, and constraints available at the time.

### Meeting Purpose

- Focus on team learning and continuous improvement  
- Avoid blaming, fostering a positive discussion environment  
- Encourage team members to communicate openly and honestly  


## 3. Gather Data

We used the collaborative document for brainstorming. Everyone recorded thoughts independently, shared their experiences during this Sprint and summarized observations from different perspectives. 

After all opinions were collected, we began discussing and analysing the thoughts.


### 1. Enjoyable - What Went Well

- Team members collaborated actively and smoothly.
- Team atmosphere was positive; tasks were completed efficiently without delays.
- Messages were replied to promptly, and issues resolved quickly.
- Team members were punctual, improving meeting and task efficiency.
- The team established clear Team Rules, which helped standardize collaboration.

### 2. Frustrating - What Was Difficult

- We didn’t have a **Product Owner** in our team, which sometimes made our task planning unclear.
- We had a short gap between Lectures and Tutorials, so we didn’t always have enough time to fully review the Lecture content before the Tutorials.
- At the project’s early stage, front-end and back-end needed more communication and coordination regarding data structure design  

### 3. Puzzling - What was confusing

- Some process details remained unclear during Sprint1. 
- Since this was our first Sprint, we weren’t confident that our final deliverables fully met the grading criteria.

### 4. Keep - What to continue doing

- We took turns being the **Scrum Master** each week. This helped everyone participate in managing the team.
- Team members responded quickly to messages, which kept our communication smooth and efficient.

### 5. More - What to do more of

- Enhance code testing processes to ensure quality after feature implementation  
- Consider taking turns on front-end and back-end tasks to improve overall technical understanding.
- Determine **User Stories** and **Acceptance Criteria** early in future Sprints  

### 6. Less - What to do less of

- Reduce some documentation work where possible to improve development efficiency  



## 4. Mute Mapping (Problem Categorization)

We categorized the feedback and summarized the main problem themes:

| Theme | Related Issues |
|-------|----------------|
| Sprint Planning & Task Definition | Process unclear, grading standards changing, task planning unclear |
| Front-end & Back-end Collaboration | Communication on data structures, testing process, and taking turns on development tasks |

After discussion and voting, we selected **Sprint Planning** as the focus for improvement in this retrospective.  

We noticed that team members were still getting familiar with the overall process, and changes in grading standards at different stages led to rework and extra time spent.

## 5. Generate Insights (Problem Analysis)

We analyzed the chosen issue further, discussing:
- Why this issue is important to our team  
- The root causes of the issue  
- Why our current workflows didn’t address the problem effectively  

### Key Findings

We realized that most of the confusion in Sprint 1 came from unclear task planning.  

- First, because we didn’t explicitly assign a **Product Owner**, Sprint goals and task descriptions were sometimes vague, making it hard for us to know if our work met expectations.  

- Second, we didn’t define User Stories and Acceptance Criteria early in the Sprint. This meant some tasks weren’t clear, which confused us and made us redo some work.


## 6. Retrospective Objective (Improvement Goal)

After discussing various options, we decided to focus on the following in the next Sprint:  

**1. Make Sprint Planning clearer.**

**2.Strengthen our communication and collaboration.**


## 7. Improvement Actions

To achieve this goal, we agreed on the following actions:

- Clearly define all **User Stories and Acceptance Criteria** during **Sprint Planning**  at early stage.
- Confirm understanding of tasks during Daily Stand-up.
- Update User Stories or Acceptance Criteria promptly when there are some changes, and make sure all team members are informed.

## 8. Follow-up Plan

We will track the execution of these improvements by:

- Checking that User Stories and Acceptance Criteria are clear during **Sprint Planning**  
- Review the issue board regularly to see if there are any ambiguous or incomplete User Stories.
- Confirm task progress during Daily Stand-up meeting, paying more attention to current blockers and discussing solutions.


## 9. Retrospective Summary
Through this retrospective, everyone had fully shared their thoughts. We've reached a consensus on directions for future improvement. 

We are confident that our workflow will be more efficient and our uncertainty will be reduced through these improvements.
